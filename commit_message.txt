Implement Distributed AI Compute (WP-101)

This commit introduces a mock protocol for distributed computing across
piOS instances. It adds a `DistributedComputeClient` to the network stack
(`userland/services/net_stack/src/distributed_compute.rs`), providing
capabilities to discover peers and share inference workloads.

Relevant tasks in `todo.md` (WP-101) have been marked as completed.
